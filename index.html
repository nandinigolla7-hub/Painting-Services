Frequently asked questions

How do I get an accurate painting quote?

Share your property details and arrange a site inspection. The final quote should consider measurements, surface condition, paint choices and preparation work.

Can I choose the paint colours?

Yes. You can shortlist colours based on your preference and lighting, then confirm the final shades before painting starts.

Does the cost calculator show the final price?

No. This demo calculator gives an illustrative starting estimate only. A confirmed quote requires a review of the property and project requirements.
